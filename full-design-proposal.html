Subject: Support Windsong Ranch Annexation to Prosper ISD - TEC 13.051

Dear Board Members, I am writing to express my strong support for the petition filed under Texas Education Code Section 13.051 to annex the Windsong Ranch area from Denton ISD to Prosper ISD. Thank you, Prosper ISD, for scheduling your December 15 hearing at 7:00 PM. THE FACTS: • All 274 students already attend Prosper ISD schools - zero attend Denton ISD • 52.6% of registered voters signed the petition - clear majority support • $6.2 million in annual tax revenue from $510 million in assessed property value • 466 homes affected, all within Prosper ISD attendance zones
TO PROSPER ISD TRUSTEES - Why This Partnership Matters: Your district's academic excellence speaks for itself: • 100% of your schools are A-rated (compared to 12.5% for Denton ISD) • 99.5% graduation rate (compared to 97.6%) • 24 National Merit Scholars (compared to 4) These Windsong Ranch families didn't choose Prosper ISD by accident - they chose excellence. They chose a district where EVERY school excels. Now they're asking to formalize what already exists: their children attend your schools, they want to be full members of your community. Why would you deny making this successful relationship permanent? These families believe in your vision, support your standards, and want to contribute their $6.2 million annually to the district their children call home. This is a win for everyone - students, families, and Prosper ISD. Please VOTE TO APPROVE this common-sense realignment.
THE PRECEDENT: This petition meets all requirements under TEC 13.051, represents the will of the majority, and would correct an unusual situation where students already attend - by contract - a different district than where their taxes go. I urge both boards to support this petition and allow these families to become full voting members of the district their children already attend. Thank you for your consideration.
