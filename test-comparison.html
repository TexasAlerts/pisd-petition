Subject: Support Windsong Ranch Annexation to Prosper ISD - TEC 13.051
Dear Board Members, I am writing to express my strong support for the petition filed under Texas Education Code Section 13.051 to annex the Windsong Ranch area from Denton ISD to Prosper ISD. Thank you, Prosper ISD, for scheduling your December 15 hearing at 7:00 PM. THE EDUCATIONAL EXCELLENCE CASE: The data tells a compelling story about why this realignment makes sense. Prosper ISD maintains an A rating (91/100) with ALL 27 schools rated A, compared to Denton ISD's B rating (80/100) with only 5 of 40 schools achieving an A rating. Prosper ISD's 99.5% graduation rate and 24 National Merit Scholars (versus Denton ISD's 97.6% and 4 scholars) demonstrate why Windsong Ranch families chose - and continue to choose - Prosper ISD for their children's education. THE FACTS: • All 274 students already attend Prosper ISD schools under contract - zero attend Denton ISD • 52.6% of registered voters signed the petition (360 of 684) - clear majority support • $6.2 million in annual tax revenue from $510 million in assessed property value • 466 homes affected, all within Prosper ISD attendance zones • This isn't just about money - it's about families having a voice in the district where their children learn THE FAIRNESS ARGUMENT: These families chose educational excellence. They chose a district where 100% of schools are A-rated. They deserve representation where their values and their children's futures align. This is about basic democratic principles - taxation WITH representation in the district that educates their children. The petition meets all requirements under TEC 13.051, has overwhelming community support, and represents a common-sense realignment. When boards review this petition, you're not just looking at boundaries on a map - you're deciding whether families who chose educational excellence deserve a voice in that excellence. I urge both boards to support this petition and allow these families to become full voting members of the district their children already attend. Thank you for your consideration.
