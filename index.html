What's Really Happening Here

  • The Legal Framework: Texas Education Code Section 13.051 provides for detachment and annexation of territory. Section 13.004 addresses debt allocation. Section 13.005 sets effective dates
  • The Financial Reality: Prosper ISD educates all 274 students but loses $6.5 million annually from $537.7 million in assessed property value to Denton ISD - funds desperately needed for our schools and future bond capacity
  • The Tax Rate Reality: With 2025-26 rates, the total tax rate difference is only $0.0072 per $100. The average homeowner pays just $83/year more ($6.92/month) to align with Prosper ISD
  • The Academic Reality: Families chose Prosper ISD where 100% of schools are A-rated (vs 12.5% for DISD), with a 99.5% graduation rate (vs 97.6%), and 24 National Merit Scholars (vs 4). Parents don't choose educational excellence by accident
  • The Student Reality: All students from these 420 homes (at build-out) attend Prosper ISD schools under the Windsong Ranch Education Agreement. Zero attend Denton ISD
  • Denton ISD Vote (Dec 9): Denied as expected. Board cited losing $6.5M in revenue — while educating zero students from this territory since 2012. Read article →
  • Prosper ISD Vote (Dec 15): APPROVED! The Board found in favor of our students' educational interests — contradicting Denton ISD's position. Read article →
  • Next Step — TEA Commissioner Appeal: Because one district approved and one denied, we now appeal to the TEA Commissioner under TEC § 13.051(j). Filing deadline: January 23, 2026. The Commissioner conducts a de novo review and makes the final decision
