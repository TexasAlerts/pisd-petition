Preview of Your Letter:

Dear Commissioner Morath,

I am writing to express my strong support for the pending appeal in the matter of the Windsong Ranch annexation petition (Docket No. TBD), filed pursuant to Texas Education Code § 13.051(j).

As a [YOUR CONNECTION], I urge you to APPROVE this petition and order the detachment of Windsong Ranch from Denton ISD and its annexation to Prosper ISD.

THE FACTS ARE CLEAR:

1. STUDENTS ALREADY ATTEND PROSPER ISD: All 274+ students from Windsong Ranch attend Prosper ISD schools. Zero students attend Denton ISD. This has been true since the subdivision was built in 2012. Access is provided via a contract between the Prosper Education Foundation (PEF), Prosper ISD, the Windsong Ranch HOA, and the developer. Documentation available at: https://prosperisdpetition.com

2. FULL EDUCATIONAL ACCESS: Prosper ISD provides full transportation, and students have complete access to all on-campus and off-campus activities. The elementary, middle, and high schools are all within walking distance. The nearest Denton ISD high school is 5+ miles away.

3. COMMUNITY SUPPORT: 52.6% of registered voters in Windsong Ranch signed this petition (360 of 684). This exceeds the statutory requirement.

4. PROSPER ISD AGREED: On December 15, 2025, Prosper ISD's Board of Trustees voted to approve the annexation, finding in favor of the students' educational interests.

5. TAX EQUITY: Denton ISD has been collecting approximately $6 million annually from Windsong Ranch for 13 years while educating none of our children. This revenue should follow the students.

THE LEGAL STANDARD SUPPORTS APPROVAL:

Texas Education Code § 13.051(h) requires you to consider "the educational interests of the students in the affected territory." Those interests are clear: these students ARE Prosper ISD students in every way except on paper.

Denton ISD's denial was driven by financial concerns — protecting $6 million in annual revenue — not by the educational interests of students. The contrasting findings from Prosper ISD demonstrate this clearly.

I respectfully request that you exercise your authority under TEC § 13.051(j) to approve this petition and set the earliest effective date permitted under TEC § 13.005(c).

Thank you for your consideration of this important matter.
