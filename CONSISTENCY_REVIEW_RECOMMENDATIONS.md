## 🔍 CRITICAL INCONSISTENCIES FOUND

### 1. **MAJOR: Two-Action Banner vs 4-Step Reality**

**Current Issue:**
- Banner says: "⚠️ YOUR SUPPORT REQUIRES **TWO ACTIONS**"
- But checklist shows: "✅ Your **4-Step** Action Checklist"

**Why This Is Confusing:**
- User sees "two actions" at top
- Then sees "four steps" below
- Creates cognitive dissonance
- Undermines the mandatory nature of sharing

**RECOMMENDATION:**
Change banner to: "⚠️ YOUR SUPPORT REQUIRES **FOUR ACTIONS**"

And update the two cards to show all 4:
```
✉️ ACTION 1: Send Email (2 min)
📅 ACTION 2: Add to Calendar
📱 ACTION 3: Share with 10+ People  ← ADD THIS
🏛️ ACTION 4: Show Up Dec 15
```

---

### 2. **Share Button Label Inconsistency**

**Current Labels (All do the same thing):**
- Countdown section: "📱 Share This Page"
- Viral section: "📱 Text Your Network"
- Post-email reminder: "📱 Share Now"
- Final reminder: "📱 Text Your Friends"

**RECOMMENDATION:**
Standardize to ONE label: **"📱 Text Your Network"**

**Why:**
- Most actionable (implies personal texts)
- Matches the "10+ people" mission
- Clear action verb
- Consistent across all locations

---

### 3. **Date Format Inconsistency**

**Current Variations:**
- "Dec 15"
- "December 15"
- "December 15, 2025"
- "Dec 15 at 7 PM"

**RECOMMENDATION:**
Standardize to: **"December 15"** (no year, no time unless needed)

**When to include time:**
- Meeting countdown section: "December 15, 2025 at 7:00 PM" (full context)
- Quick references: "December 15" (everywhere else)

---

### 4. **Target Audience List Format**

**Current Variations:**
- "friends, family, neighbors" (lowercase, commas)
- "Friends • Family • Neighbors • Coworkers • Fellow Parents" (title case, bullets)
- "Friends, family, neighbors" (mixed case)

**RECOMMENDATION:**
Standardize to: **"Friends • Family • Neighbors • Coworkers • Prosper ISD Parents"**

**Why:**
- Consistent bullet format
- Title case (more professional)
- Specific to audience (Prosper ISD Parents vs generic "Fellow Parents")

---

### 5. **Urgency Language Mix**

**Current Terms Used:**
- "MANDATORY"
- "REQUIRED"
- "CRITICAL"
- "Must"

**RECOMMENDATION:**
Primary term: **"REQUIRED"**
Secondary: **"CRITICAL"** (for extra emphasis)

**Usage Guide:**
- Headers/titles: "REQUIRED"
- Body text explaining why: "CRITICAL"
- Avoid: "MANDATORY" (sounds dictatorial)
- Avoid: "Must" (softer, less urgent)

---

### 6. **Share Mission Statement Inconsistency**

**Current Variations:**
- "Share with EVERYONE You Know"
- "Share with at least 10 people"
- "Share with 10+ people"

**RECOMMENDATION:**
Standardize to: **"Share with 10+ People"**

**Usage:**
- Headlines: "Share with 10+ People"
- Body text: "Share with at least 10 people in your network"
- Always reference the specific target (10+)
