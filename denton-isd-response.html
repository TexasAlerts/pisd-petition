Appealing to TEA Commissioner

Following Denton ISD's denial on December 9, 2025 and Prosper ISD's approval on December 15, 2025, we are now appealing to the TEA Commissioner under TEC § 13.051(j).

December 16, 2025
Dr. Susannah Holbert O'Bara, Superintendent
Denton Independent School District
1307 N. Locust Street
Denton, TX 76201
RE: Request for Documentation Following December 9, 2025 Denial — Windsong Ranch Annexation Petition
Dear Dr. O'Bara and Members of the Board of Trustees,

Thank you for scheduling a public hearing and considering the Windsong Ranch annexation petition on December 9, 2025. While the Board's decision to deny the petition was disappointing to our community, we respect the process and appreciate the opportunity to present our case.

Prosper ISD's Contrary Findings

As discussed in my testimony before your Board, and as anticipated, Prosper ISD voted to approve our annexation petition on December 15, 2025. Prosper ISD's Board of Trustees made findings of fact regarding the educational interests of our 274 students that are contrary to the positions articulated by Denton ISD.

Under Texas Education Code § 13.051(j), because one affected district approved and one disapproved the petition, we are entitled to appeal to the Commissioner of Education. This appeal is de novo, meaning the Commissioner will conduct an independent review of all facts and is not bound by either district's findings.

Request for Documentation

To prepare our appeal to the TEA Commissioner, I respectfully request the following documentation from Denton ISD:

  1. Formal Motion and Vote Record: The official motion made by the Board, including the exact language of the motion and the recorded vote (including any dissents or abstentions).
  2. Findings of Fact: Any written findings of fact adopted by the Board in support of the denial, including the specific criteria considered under TEC § 13.051(h) regarding:
    • The educational interests of the students in the affected territory and the affected districts
    • The social, economic, and educational effects of the proposed boundary change
  3. Meeting Minutes: Official minutes from the December 9, 2025 board meeting reflecting the discussion and action taken on the annexation petition.
  4. Staff Report or Recommendation: Any staff analysis, recommendation, or report provided to the Board regarding the petition.
  5. Written Denial Notice: Any formal written notice of denial as may be required under applicable law or district policy.

Timeline

Under 19 TAC § 157.1051, we must file our Petition for Review with the TEA within 45 calendar days of the challenged action. Our filing deadline is January 23, 2026.

To ensure adequate time to prepare a complete appeal, I respectfully request that the documentation listed above be provided by January 6, 2026.
