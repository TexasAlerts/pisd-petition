PETITION FOR REVIEW

TO THE HONORABLE COMMISSIONER OF EDUCATION:

Petitioners, residents of the Windsong Ranch subdivision located within the boundaries of Denton Independent School District ("Denton ISD"), file this Petition for Review pursuant to Texas Education Code § 13.051(j) and 19 TAC § 157.1051, and respectfully show the Commissioner the following:

I. Description of Challenged Action

1. On December 9, 2025, the Board of Trustees of Denton Independent School District voted to deny a petition for detachment and annexation filed by residents of the Windsong Ranch subdivision pursuant to Texas Education Code § 13.051.

2. The petition sought to detach the Windsong Ranch subdivision (the "Territory") from Denton ISD and annex it to Prosper Independent School District ("Prosper ISD").

3. On December 15, 2025, the Board of Trustees of Prosper Independent School District voted to approve the same petition for detachment and annexation.

4. Because one affected district approved and one affected district disapproved the petition, Petitioners are entitled to appeal to the Commissioner of Education under Texas Education Code § 13.051(j).

II. Date of Challenged Action

5. The challenged action—Denton ISD's denial of the annexation petition—occurred on December 9, 2025.

6. This Petition for Review is filed within 45 calendar days of that decision, as required by 19 TAC § 157.1051.

III. Relief Requested

7. Petitioners respectfully request that the Commissioner of Education:

  • Reverse the decision of Denton ISD's Board of Trustees denying the annexation petition;
  • Approve the detachment of the Windsong Ranch subdivision from Denton ISD and its annexation to Prosper ISD;
  • Set an effective date for the transfer that is the soonest date permitted by law under Texas Education Code § 13.005(c), which provides that the transfer shall be effective on "a date set by the commissioner that is not earlier than the 30th day after the date of the commissioner's decision"; and
  • Grant such other relief as the Commissioner deems just and proper.

IV. Statement of Jurisdiction and Legal Basis

8. The Commissioner has jurisdiction over this appeal pursuant to Texas Education Code § 13.051(j), which provides:

"If the board of trustees of only one affected district disapproves the petition, an aggrieved party to the proceedings in either district may appeal the board's decision to the commissioner under Section 7.057. An appeal under this subsection is de novo."

9. The Commissioner's authority to decide this appeal is further established by Texas Education Code § 7.057, which grants the Commissioner authority to hear appeals from school district board decisions.

10. This appeal is governed by 19 TAC Chapter 157, Subchapter EE (Hearings and Appeals), which establishes procedures for Commissioner appeals.

11. Pursuant to Texas Education Code § 13.051(j), this appeal is de novo, meaning the Commissioner shall conduct an independent review of the facts and is not bound by Denton ISD's findings or conclusions.

12. In deciding this appeal, the Commissioner shall consider, pursuant to Texas Education Code § 13.051(h):

  • The educational interests of the students in the affected territory and the affected districts; and
  • The social, economic, and educational effects of the proposed boundary change.

V. Facts Supporting Approval

13. Student Educational Interests: 274 students from Windsong Ranch currently attend Prosper ISD schools under transfer agreements. These students have never attended Denton ISD schools. The nearest Denton ISD schools are more than 10 miles away, while Prosper ISD schools are within walking distance of the Territory.

14. Community Support: The petition was signed by 360 registered voters, representing 52.6% of the 684 registered voters residing in the Territory. This exceeds the statutory requirement of a majority under Texas Education Code § 13.051(b).

15. Tax Revenue: The Territory generates approximately $6.5 million annually in school property tax revenue. For 13 years, this revenue has gone to Denton ISD, which has educated zero students from the Territory during that entire period.

16. Property Value: The Territory contains approximately 466 homes with an estimated assessed property value of $537.7 million.

17. Community Integration: Windsong Ranch residents are fully integrated into the Prosper community. Their children attend Prosper ISD schools, participate in Prosper extracurricular activities, and the subdivision is contiguous with other Prosper ISD territory.

18. Receiving District Approval: Prosper ISD's Board of Trustees unanimously approved the annexation petition on December 15, 2025, demonstrating the receiving district's willingness and capacity to serve these students.
