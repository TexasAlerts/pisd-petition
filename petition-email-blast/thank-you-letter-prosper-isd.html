Next Steps — TEA Commissioner Appeal

As you know, Denton ISD denied our petition on December 9, 2025. Under Texas Education Code § 13.051(j), because one affected district approved and one disapproved, we have the right to appeal to the Commissioner of Education. The appeal must be filed within 45 calendar days of Denton ISD's denial.

Filing Deadline: January 23, 2026

The Appeal Process

Step Timeline Citation
File Petition for Review with TEA By January 23, 2026 19 TAC § 157.1051
Denton ISD files Answer + Local Record 30 days after docketing 19 TAC § 157.1052
ALJ issues Proposal for Decision 180 days from filing TEC § 7.057(b)

Motion for Rehearing (optional) 25 days after decision 19 TAC § 157.1061
Appeal to Travis County District Court (optional) After rehearing resolved TEC § 7.057(d)

Standard of Review

Importantly, Texas Education Code § 13.051(j) specifies that this appeal is de novo, meaning the Commissioner conducts an independent review of the facts and is not bound by Denton ISD's reasoning. The Commissioner must consider:

  • The educational interests of the students in the affected territory and the affected districts; and
  • The social, economic, and educational effects of the proposed boundary change.

(Texas Education Code § 13.051(h))

Effective Date

Under Texas Education Code § 13.005(c), if the Commissioner approves the annexation, the transfer becomes effective on "a date set by the commissioner that is not earlier than the 30th day after the date of the commissioner's decision."

Unlike standard annexations (which must be effective on July 1), Commissioner appeals provide flexibility. We will request the soonest effective date permitted by law.

Realistic Timeline: If we file by late January 2026 and receive a decision within the 240-day statutory period (approximately September 2026), the earliest effective date would be October 2026. The Commissioner may choose to align with a fiscal year boundary (January 1, 2027) or school year (July 1, 2027) for administrative convenience.
