# PISD Board Speech - Doug Charles
## Monday, December 15, 2025 | 7 Minutes

---

## OPENING (30 seconds)

Good evening, President McLaughlin, Trustees, Dr. Crownover, and members of the administration.

My name is Doug Charles. I live at 4360 Mill Branch Drive in Windsong Ranch. I'm a Senior Vice President at Rocket Mortgage, where I oversee Business Innovation, Technology, and Data Analytics.

My family chose Prosper over 20 years ago. Our three children graduated from Prosper ISD schools. When we built in Windsong Ranch, we did so specifically to remain in Prosper ISD.

Tonight, I'm presenting on behalf of 360 families who signed this petition — 52.6% of registered voters in our community.

---

## THE FRAMEWORK: WHAT THE LAW REQUIRES (45 seconds)

Before I present our case, let me share what Texas Education Code Section 13.051 requires you and the TEA Commissioner to consider:

**Three questions:**

1. **What are the educational interests of the students in the affected territory?**
2. **What are the educational interests of students in the affected districts?**
3. **What are the social, economic, and educational effects of this boundary change?**

I'll answer each of these questions with verified data — and I encourage you to ask the administration these same questions.

---

## QUESTION 1: Educational Interests of Windsong Students (60 seconds)

**All 274 students from Windsong Ranch already attend Prosper ISD schools.**

- They walk to Windsong Ranch Elementary
- They attend Reynolds Middle School
- They're Eagles at Prosper High School

Zero students attend Denton ISD. Zero. Since 2012.

The nearest Denton ISD school is over 10 miles away. Our children would have to drive past multiple Prosper schools to reach a Denton school.

**What do our students gain from annexation?**
- Permanent enrollment — no more transfer agreements that must be renewed
- Parents gain voting rights in PISD board elections — the people who govern their children's education
- Stability and permanence for current and future families

**What do they lose?** Nothing. They're already here.

---

## QUESTION 2: Educational Interests of PISD Students (90 seconds)

This is where I need to address the financial narrative directly.

**Our independent financial analysis shows this annexation is fiscally beneficial.**

We built a model using current TEA school finance formulas, 2025-26 adopted tax rates, and Denton CAD certified property values:

| Category | Amount |
|----------|--------|
| **New M&O Revenue** | $3,840,000 |
| **New I&S Revenue** | $2,688,000 |
| **Total New Revenue** | **$6.5 Million** |
| | |
| **Less: Student Education Costs** | -$3,700,000 |
| **Less: Additional Recapture** | -$310,000 |
| **Less: Denton Debt Share (Year 1)** | -$2,490,000 |
| | |
| **Year 1 Net Benefit** | **$3.73 Million** |

But here's what changes after Year 12 when the Denton debt is paid off:

**Annual Net Benefit (Year 13+)**: **$9+ Million per year**

**30-Year Cumulative Net Benefit: $248 Million**

This analysis uses official TEA formulas, including full recapture modeling. We encourage you to verify these figures with your administration.

---

## QUESTION 3: Social, Economic & Educational Effects (90 seconds)

**Social Impact:** Your administration stated — and I quote — "There is no negative social impact anticipated."

**Economic Impact:**
- $537.7 million in assessed property value added to PISD's tax base
- Future bond capacity for building schools
- Tax dollars following students to the schools that educate them

**The Alternative — What Happens If You Vote No:**

PISD continues to educate 274 students while receiving **zero local tax dollars** from their families.

That means every other Prosper ISD taxpayer subsidizes our children's education.

**Is that fair to YOUR constituents?**

Meanwhile, Denton ISD collects $6.5 million annually from Windsong Ranch while educating zero of our students.

That's not a broken system. That's a system working exactly as designed — just not for students or taxpayers.

---

## ADDRESSING THE CONCERNS (60 seconds)

I understand there are concerns beyond the financials. Let me address them directly:

**"What about PEF?"**

I spoke with PEF leadership this week. They confirmed:
- PEF always considered Windsong Ranch a **declining revenue source** that might go away
- PEF has $3.27 million in assets and receives $1.65 million in annual contributions
- PEF will survive and thrive — they always have

**"What about precedent?"**

This isn't a loophole. This is Texas law working as intended. Chapter 13 exists precisely for situations like this — where district boundaries no longer make sense for students and taxpayers.

**"What about 466 new voters?"**

These families already send their children to your schools. They already support your bond elections. They already volunteer in your classrooms.

Shouldn't they have a voice in the governance of their children's education?

---

## THE FIDUCIARY QUESTION (45 seconds)

I'll close with this:

You have a fiduciary duty to the financial health of Prosper ISD.

The data shows this annexation generates:
- $6.5 million in Year 1 revenue
- Net positive returns even after all costs
- $248 million over 30 years (our model with full recapture)

Voting NO means:
- Continuing to educate students without local tax support
- Leaving $248 million on the table over 30 years
- Forcing other PISD taxpayers to subsidize our children

**A NO vote is not fiscally responsible. It's not what's best for PISD students. And it's not what the law intends.**

---

## CLOSING (30 seconds)

Twenty years ago, my family chose Prosper. Three graduations later, we're still here — still committed to this community.

360 families — a majority of our voters — are asking you to do what's right:
- Right for our children
- Right for PISD taxpayers
- Right for the financial health of this district

We respectfully ask for your YES vote tonight.

Thank you. I'm happy to answer questions.

---

## KEY QUESTIONS TO ASK THE ADMINISTRATION

If trustees want to probe the financial impact, consider asking:

1. **"What is the 30-year cumulative net revenue from this annexation?"**
   - Our independent model shows ~$248 million

2. **"After the Denton debt is paid off in Year 12, what is the annual net benefit?"**
   - Our model shows ~$9 million/year

3. **"What is the cost to PISD of continuing to educate these students without annexation?"**
   - Answer: They're already in our ADA count. We educate them regardless. Annexation just brings the revenue.

4. **"Is there any negative social impact anticipated from this annexation?"**
   - We see none — these students are already fully integrated in PISD schools

5. **"Does TEA consider financial impact when deciding annexation appeals?"**
   - Yes — economic effects are explicitly listed in TEC 13.051
